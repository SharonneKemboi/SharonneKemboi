## 🔎 Research Direction

### Data Silences & Inclusive AI

I am interested in the spaces between **what exists in the world and what becomes represented in data**.

A central question guiding my work is:

> **Who is missing? What is missing? Where are the gaps? And what happens when computational systems learn from those gaps?**

I want to explore reproducible approaches for:

1. Identifying gaps and underrepresentation in datasets
2. Measuring data quality and completeness
3. Understanding how missingness affects models and predictions
4. Quantifying uncertainty and limitations
5. Examining how inequality can propagate through computational systems
6. Designing more inclusive and accountable data and AI systems

## 🌱 Building Towards

I am working towards a research career focused on **AI, data science and responsible computational systems**.

My aim is to develop the technical depth to build advanced AI systems while maintaining a critical understanding of the social contexts, assumptions and inequalities embedded within data.

Ultimately, I want my research to help answer a simple but important question:

> **How can we build intelligent systems that do not become more intelligent at reproducing the inequalities already present in society?**
